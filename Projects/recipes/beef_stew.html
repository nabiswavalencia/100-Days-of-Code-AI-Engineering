Beef Stew Recipe

A hearty and comforting beef stew recipe that is perfect for chilly days.

This recipe combines tender chunks of beef with a rich and flavorful broth, along with vegetables like carrots, potatoes, and onions.

It is a classic dish that can be served with crusty bread or over rice for a complete meal.

Enjoy the warmth and deliciousness of this homemade beef stew!

Ingredients:

  • 2 pounds beef stew meat, cut into chunks
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 cups beef broth
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
  • 4 large carrots, sliced
  • 4 large potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat the olive oil over medium-high heat. Add the beef chunks and brown on all sides. Remove the beef and set aside.
  2. Add the chopped onion to the pot and sauté until softened, about 5 minutes. Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute.
  3. Return the browned beef to the pot. Stir in the tomato paste, thyme, and rosemary.
  4. Add the beef broth and water to the pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer for 1 hour.
  5. Add the sliced carrots and cubed potatoes to the pot. Continue to simmer for another 30-40 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot and enjoy your hearty beef stew!
